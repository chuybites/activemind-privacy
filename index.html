Privacy Policy

ActiveMind · Last updated: October 2026

The short version

You don't need an account to use ActiveMind. What you log (moods, journal, check-ins, your streak) stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you keep backup on. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

ActiveMind has one small server, and it is only for things between people: sharing a day trip as a link, and a circle of friends. It is used only if you choose to do one of those things. It never holds your moods, journal or places. From Health it holds only the few numbers you switch on for your circle, one switch each, off until you turn it on.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name and goals, mood check-ins, journal entries, activities and trips you finish, your streak, and your settings are stored on your iPhone. If you delete the app, they are removed from the phone.

iCloud backup

On unless you turn it off in Settings. Your streak, moods, journal and profile are copied to your own iCloud account, so a new phone starts where you left off. Apple stores it in your account; ActiveMind never sees it.

Apple Health

Only with your permission, which you can change any time in Settings › Health › ActiveMind.

  • Reads: your activity (steps, distance, energy, exercise, stand hours, flights, workouts), heart rate, resting heart rate and HRV, sleep, breathing rate and blood oxygen, wrist temperature, walking steadiness and gait, VO2 max, time in daylight, past mindful minutes and moods, and your date of birth (for fitness age and your birthday; asked only if you connect Health in onboarding or Settings).
  • Writes: Mindful Minutes when you finish a check-in, a session or an activity, and State of Mind when you log how you feel (including through Siri).

Health data is used to show you your own readings in the app. Only if you have a circle and turn on a switch for it in the circle's "What you share", one number a day of that kind goes to ActiveMind's server for your friends' points and challenges: your step total, one sleep score a night (never its stages), or your active minutes. Turn a switch off and what it shared is removed. Steps typed into Health by hand never count. Nothing else from Health (heart rate, HRV, stages, breathing, temperature, weight, moods) ever leaves your iPhone, and none of it is used for advertising.

Location

Used for weather, warnings, nearby places and day trips while the app is open, and, if you keep Severe weather notifications on, by a check iOS runs now and then in the background, which sends where you last were, rounded to about a kilometre, to the National Weather Service. Your coordinates are sent, with nothing else attached, to the services that answer:

  • Open-Meteo for weather
  • The National Weather Service for weather warnings, in the United States
  • The EPA's AirNow for air quality, in the United States, rounded to about seven miles
  • OpenStreetMap for opening hours and nearby courts and restrooms
  • The National Park Service for parks
  • Wikipedia for what a place is
  • Apple Maps, which draws the maps, including the one on a trip's share card

Kept on your iPhone: where today's trips were planned from, and the places you finish trips at, which is what draws your Discovery map.

Your account (optional)

Only needed to share a trip as a link or to have a circle of friends. You sign in with Apple; there is no password. ActiveMind's server, run on Supabase in the United States, then keeps:

  • the sign-in identifier Apple gives it
  • an email address from Apple, which is a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email
  • the name and emoji you pick
  • if you add friends, your profile card as you fill it in: a colour and pattern, a second emoji, a title, up to three tags, three short answers, three badges and three numbers you choose (such as your streak), four favourites and a short line about what you are up to on its back, one thing you showcase (a record, a crown, a day trip or a badge; a record carries its number only when that reading is switched on under What you share), and how the front is laid out; each part can be kept to yourself
  • if you make a face in the app and choose to wear it instead of your emoji, that face as a short code (its skin tone, hairstyle, hair colour, eyes, mouth, and whether it has glasses or a beard), so your friends see it on your card; switching back to your emoji removes it
  • if you allow notifications from your circle, your iPhone's push address, whether friend notifications are on, your quiet hours and your time zone, so a message or invite can reach you; removed when you sign out

Nothing you log goes there. You can delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete my account, which removes it and everything linked to it (trip links, your profile card, your circle and everything you sent friends) from the server. What is on your iPhone stays.

Trip links

Only when you send one. The day goes to ActiveMind's server as you see it: its stops, times, addresses and map, with your name and emoji. Where you planned it from is left off. Anyone with the link can see that one day, and nothing else of yours, on a web page and in the app, for 30 days or until you stop it in Settings › Account › Trip links. The web page shows the map using OpenStreetMap.

Your circle

Only if you add friends, up to five, each with an invite link. Your friends see your profile card, a status you set (for a day), your streak, which days you moved in the last 90 (an activity done, a day trip finished, or 5,000 steps), the cheers, notes, plans, postcards and pokes you send them, and your answer to the week's question once they have answered too. Friends can see when you've read their messages, unless you turn Read receipts off in Circle settings; then they see only that it arrived. A postcard carries a finished day trip's name, stops and date, never where you set out from. Never your moods, journal or places. Messages are cleared after 60 days, and moved days after 90. Either of you can remove the other at any time, which removes what you sent each other.

Points, crowns and challenges

Each phone works out the circle's points from the same shared days, so the server keeps no totals. For each day it holds only what you switched on (see Apple Health), how many activities you finished and trip stops you ticked, how many kind things you did (messages, comments, reactions and ticks on a friend's promise, cut to what was really sent), and a rest day you planned. Your friends see those numbers for the weeks they can see on the board. Crowns, titles and records are worked out on each phone.

In a challenge, your phone sends one number for its window (steps, active minutes, a sleep score, or whether you were asleep by your bedtime), and everyone in it sees everyone's number for that challenge. A challenge opened with a link can include up to ten people who are not friends with each other: they see each other's name, emoji and numbers in that challenge only.

Trips together

Only when you plan a day trip with friends. The trip goes to the server as it does in a link, without where you planned it from, with who is in, which days suit each person, the day it is on, when each person ticked each stop, a "running late" note for an hour, votes to keep or swap a stop, and the trip's own thread. Only the people on the trip see it. It is removed two months after its day, or when everyone leaves.

The circle feed

Only what you choose to post, with one switch each for activities you finish, trips you finish and your wins (records, crowns, challenges, streaks and unlocks), each off until you turn it on. A moment holds its name, an emoji and a short line, and never a number from Health. Your friends see your moments. Reactions and comments on a moment are seen by the poster and the poster's friends, with each person's name and emoji, so a friend of your friend can see your comment there. You can mention someone who can see the moment. You can take down your own moments and comments, and comments on your moments. Moments are kept two months.

Being there

Only if you use them. A pact between two friends is seen by the two of you only: its goal, its days, and each day's check-in with a short note if you add one; it is removed a month after it ends. Your promise for the week, and which friends said you kept it, is seen by your friends and removed after eight weeks. Support mode tells your friends you are taking it easy until a day you choose, never why, and takes you off the week's board. Asking your circle for a push sends one message to each friend, once a day.

Muting, reporting and blocking

Muting a friend happens on your iPhone only, and they are not told. When you report something, ActiveMind's server keeps your report with a copy of what was said, who said it and who reported it, so it can be looked at even if it is deleted; the person is not told who reported them. When you block someone, the friendship and what you sent each other are removed, and neither of you can invite the other or join the other's challenge rooms; the server keeps who you blocked until you unblock them. A short list of hurtful words is refused anywhere people write to each other in ActiveMind. To report something urgent, write to the address under Contact.

Circle rules

Before you take part in a circle, you agree to keep it kind: no bullying, threats, hate or harassment; nothing sexual, violent or cruel; and only what is yours to share. ActiveMind has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Reports are read within a day, and anything that breaks these rules is removed, along with the account that posted it.

Calendar

Only when you put a day trip in it, or a plan with a friend you said yes to. ActiveMind adds those events, moves them when you swap a stop and removes them when you take the trip out, and keeps a note of which events it made. If you turn on Find my free time (it is off until you do), ActiveMind also reads when you are busy, meaning the start and end of your events and whether they mark you busy, on your phone, to suggest times for a day out. It never reads titles, places, people or notes, nothing from your calendar leaves your phone, and it never changes anything else in your calendar.

Notifications

Planned on your iPhone, three a day at most: a morning plan, a move nudge only if you haven't moved, and wind-down before bed, plus moments like a trip alert, a challenge's last hour, or something new from your circle. When a friend sends you something, the server can ask Apple to show it on your iPhone right away; it never does in your quiet hours. Choose which in Settings › Notifications, or turn them all off in iOS Settings. If you answer wind-down with a mood by pressing and holding it, that mood is saved like any other check-in.

Widgets and Siri

Your readiness, sleep, stress, steps and a trip's next stop are copied from the app into a space on your iPhone that only ActiveMind's own widgets, Lock Screen countdown and Siri shortcuts can read. Nothing leaves the phone.

Share cards

A card you share is drawn on your iPhone and goes only where you send it. Save Image adds it to your photo library when you choose it; ActiveMind never reads your photos.

Analytics, advertising and crash reports

ActiveMind does not collect analytics or usage data, use advertising frameworks, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share your data. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on in Settings. When on, a crash sends what went wrong and where in the code to Sentry, never your writing, moods, places, Health data or who you are.

Your choices

  • Export everything in Settings › Privacy gives you your data as a file, including everything the server holds when you have an account.
  • Delete all my data in Settings › Privacy wipes it from your iPhone.
  • Delete my account in Settings › Account removes it from the server.

Children's privacy

ActiveMind is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It can be used fully without an account.
